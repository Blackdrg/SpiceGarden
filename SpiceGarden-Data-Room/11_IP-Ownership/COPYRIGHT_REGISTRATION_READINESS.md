## 1. Statutory Framework (India)

Software copyright registration in India is governed by:
* **The Copyright Act, 1957** (as amended), specifically:
  - Section 2(o): Definition of "literary work" (includes computer programmes, tables, and compilations including computer databases).
  - Section 2(ffc): Definition of "computer programme" ("a set of instructions expressed in words, codes, schemes or in any other form, including a machine readable medium, capable of causing a computer to perform a particular task or achieve a particular result").
  - Section 14(b): Exclusive rights in respect of a computer programme.
  - Section 17: First owner of copyright (work made in the course of employment / contract of service / assignment).
  - Section 19: Mode of assignment (written assignment signed by assignor).
  - Section 44–50: Register of Copyrights and Registration Procedure.
* **The Copyright Rules, 2013** (Form XIV — Application for Registration of Copyright, Statement of Particulars, Statement of Further Particulars).

## 5. Next Steps for Legal Counsel & Owner

1. **Counsel Review:** Have Indian IP counsel review Form XIV particulars.
2. **Portal Submission:** Log in to `copyright.gov.in` e-filing portal and complete Form XIV.
3. **Statutory Fee:** Pay statutory filing fee (₹500 for Individual/Startup/Literary Computer Software).
4. **Physical Copy Submission:** Dispatch signed Form XIV, Statement of Particulars, Statement of Further Particulars, Power of Attorney/NOC (if represented by advocate), and physical optical disc / print deposit (first & last 10 pages of source code) to the Copyright Office, New Delhi within 30 days of e-filing.
