## 1. Identified Brand Risks and Mitigation Strategies

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TM-R01** | **Physical Restaurant Name Confusion (Class 43):** Local brick-and-mortar restaurants or catering services may operate under variations of "Spice Garden". | Moderate | Moderate | Restrict trademark claims primarily to **Class 09 (Software)** and **Class 42 (SaaS Technology Platform)**; include descriptive disclaimers regarding software platform nature vs restaurant dining. |
| **TM-R02** | **Descriptive Objection under Section 9:** Trademark examiner may raise preliminary objection that "Spice" + "Garden" contains dictionary words. | Low | Moderate | File with evidence of composite arbitrary combination, stylized logo emblem, and distinct digital application context. |
| **TM-R03** | **International Extension (Madrid System):** Potential third-party filings in target expansion countries (UAE, Singapore, US, UK). | Moderate | Low | File Madrid Protocol international application designating priority from initial Indian application within 6 months of domestic filing. |
| **TM-R04** | **Squatting of Secondary TLDs:** Third parties acquiring `.in`, `.co.in`, or `.delivery` domains. | Low | Moderate | Preemptively register defensive ccTLDs (`spicegarden.in`, `spicegarden.co.in`) upon commercial entity setup. |
| **TM-R05** | **Failure to Promptly File:** Delay in filing allows third parties to file identical marks in technology class. | High | Low | Promptly execute Form TM-A filing via statutory portal. |
