## 1. Trademark Diligence Classification Framework

To maintain strict legal accuracy, SpiceGarden distinguishes four distinct stages of brand protection:

| Stage | Definition | Current SpiceGarden Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Preliminary Search** | Internal public database query on IP India and global registers to spot direct identical name clashes. | **COMPLETED** (Zero direct identical conflicts in primary classes) |
| **Stage 2: Legal Clearance** | Formal search and legal opinion by qualified trademark attorney assessing phonetic, visual, and conceptual similarity risks. | **PENDING COUNSEL REVIEW** |
| **Stage 3: Application Filed** | Formal electronic filing of Form TM-A with the Trade Marks Registry (receipt of Application Number). | **READY FOR FILING** (`USER ACTION REQUIRED`) |
| **Stage 4: Registration Granted** | Examination completed, published in TM Journal without opposition, and Certificate of Registration issued. | **NOT GRANTED** (Requires statutory process) |

> [!CAUTION]
> SpiceGarden makes **NO claim of existing registered trademark rights**. All brand diligence is documented at the preliminary search and filing readiness stage.

## 2. Applicable Nice Classification Classes

1. **Class 09 (Software & Digital Goods):**
   * *Specification:* Downloadable computer software; downloadable mobile application software for ordering food, dispatching delivery drivers, tracking orders in real-time, kitchen display systems, and processing restaurant payments.
2. **Class 39 (Logistics & Delivery Services):**
   * *Specification:* Transportation services; delivery of food and beverages; courier services; hyper-local parcel logistics; tracking of delivery vehicles by computer.
3. **Class 42 (SaaS & Technical Platform Services):**
   * *Specification:* Software as a Service (SaaS) and Platform as a Service (PaaS) featuring software for restaurant operations, menu management, order dispatching, driver routing, and transaction settlement.
4. **Class 43 (Restaurant Aggregation Services):**
   * *Specification:* Providing food ordering services on behalf of restaurants; restaurant information and customer review services.
