# Customer Success Guide

**Version:** 1.0.0

## Onboarding
- Welcome sequence; MFA prompt; preference center.
- Merchants/drivers: guided KYC + first-order/first-delivery.

## Health metrics
- Activation (first order), retention (28-day), NPS, refund rate.
- At-risk: no order in 30 days → re-engagement.

## Playbook
- Proactive outreach on friction (failed payment, repeated cancellation).
- Quarterly business review for enterprise merchants.
