# Pricing Documentation (Template)

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** TEMPLATE — Finance to populate final numbers. DO NOT cite externally until approved.

## Model (structure)
- **Commission:** % of order subtotal, per agreed rate card (see Merchant Agreement §Commission Policy).
- **Subscription (optional):** monthly per restaurant for premium features (analytics, loyalty engine).
- **Delivery fee:** distance + demand, shown at checkout (Delivery Policy).
- **Wallet:** stored-value; refunds may credit wallet at user choice (Refund Policy).

## Rate cards
- Defined per merchant in the merchant dashboard; not public.

## Invoicing & tax
- GST computed per transaction (Merchant Agreement §Tax).
- Settlement on T+[N] cycle to registered bank.
