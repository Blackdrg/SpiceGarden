# FAQ (Customer)

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Q: Where is my order?**
A: Open the app → Orders for live status and ETA.

**Q: How do I get a refund?**
A: Cancel before restaurant acceptance for full refund; otherwise request via the order → Refund Policy applies (5–10 business days to original method, or wallet credit).

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: Privacy Dashboard → request deletion, or email privacy@spicegarden.com (GDPR/DPDP right).

**Q: How do I opt out of marketing?**
A: Privacy Dashboard or unsubscribe links; consent manageable per-category.

**Q: Is my payment info safe?**
A: We never store card numbers; payments are tokenized via PCI-DSS processors (Stripe/Razorpay).
