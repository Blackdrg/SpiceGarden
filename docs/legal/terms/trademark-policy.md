## 1. Trademark Rights

SpiceGarden Technologies Pvt. Ltd. owns trademarks including but not limited to:
- "SpiceGarden" and the SpiceGarden logo
- Application icons and UI elements
- Marketing slogans and brand colors
- Domain names (spicegarden.com, etc.)

These marks are protected under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 (India) and international trademark laws.

## 2. Permitted Use

You may use SpiceGarden trademarks only as follows:
- Referencing SpiceGarden as your technology provider (for merchants, delivery partners, and partners)
- Linking to SpiceGarden platforms from your website or marketing materials
- Using the standard attribution: "Powered by SpiceGarden"

All use must be in accordance with our brand guidelines, which are available at `brand.spicegarden.com`.

## 3. Prohibited Use

You must NOT:
- Use SpiceGarden marks in a way that implies endorsement or partnership beyond the actual relationship
- Modify SpiceGarden marks
- Use SpiceGarden marks as part of your own trademarks, domain names, or company names
- Use SpiceGarden marks in marketing materials without prior written approval
- Register domain names or social media accounts that incorporate SpiceGarden marks

## 4. Brand Guidelines

All use of SpiceGarden marks must follow the official brand guidelines, including:
- Minimum clear space around marks
- Color and background restrictions
- Typography and spacing
- Prohibited variations

## 5. Infringement

If we become aware of unauthorized use of our trademarks, we may:
1. Request removal or correction
2. Send a cease and desist letter
3. Pursue legal action

We will make reasonable efforts to contact you before taking legal action.

## 6. Domain Name Disputes

If you register, traffic in, or use a domain name that is identical or confusingly similar to a SpiceGarden trademark, we may request transfer or cancellation of the domain under ICANN's Uniform Domain-Name Dispute-Resolution Policy (UDRP) or applicable local law.
