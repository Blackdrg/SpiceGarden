The SpiceGarden Help Center provides self-service support resources for customers, delivery partners, and restaurant staff.

## 3. Common Customer Issues

### Order Not Received
1. Check order status in the app under "My Orders"
2. Contact the delivery partner via the app's chat
3. If no response within 15 minutes, escalate to support
4. Support can track the order via the driver's GPS location

### Wrong Order
1. Report via "Help > Order Issue" within 30 minutes of delivery
2. Provide photo of delivered items
3. Refund or replacement processed within 24 hours

### Payment Issue
1. Check bank statement for the transaction
2. Payment may take 24-48 hours to reflect
3. Contact payment@spicegarden.com for disputes

### Account Deletion
Customers can request data deletion:
```
POST /compliance/gdpr/user/:userId/deletion-request
```
Requests are processed within 24 hours, with full deletion completed within 30 days.

## 4. Common Delivery Partner Issues

### App Not Working
1. Restart the app
2. Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > SpiceGarden > Storage > Clear Cache)
3. Check for app updates in the app store
4. Contact driver-support@spicegarden.com

### OTP Not Received
1. Check SMS inbox for OTP
2. Request a new OTP via "Resend OTP" in the app
3. Verify phone number is correct in profile
4. Contact support if OTP not received after 3 attempts

### Cash Reconciliation
Delivery partners handling COD must submit daily cash reconciliation through the partner dashboard.

## 5. Common Restaurant Issues

### Order Not Appearing
1. Check internet connection
2. Restart the restaurant dashboard app
3. Verify restaurant is marked as "active" in the admin panel
4. Contact restaurant-support@spicegarden.com

### Menu Changes Not Reflecting
Menu changes take 5-10 minutes to propagate to customer apps after saving.

## 8. Escalation

If support cannot resolve your issue within 24 hours:
1. Request escalation via in-app chat
2. A senior support agent will contact you within 2 hours
3. If unresolved in 48 hours, a manager will review your case
