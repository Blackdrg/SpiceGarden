# BYOD Policy (Bring Your Own Device)

**Version:** 1.0.0

## Scope
Personal devices used to access company resources.

## Requirements
- Disk encryption on; OS patched; screen lock.
- Managed MDM profile for email/app access; containerized work data.
- No local copies of user PII outside the managed container.

## Separation & Exit
- Remote wipe of managed container on exit/loss.
- Personal data untouched by wipe.
