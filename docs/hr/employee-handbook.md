## 1. Welcome
SpiceGarden builds a multi-tenant food-delivery platform. This handbook summarizes expectations and resources.

## 2. Employment Basics
- Working hours, leave, and holidays per local law and offer letter.
- Payroll, benefits, and tax (Form 16 / 10x) handled by HRIS.

## 3. Code of Conduct
- Act with integrity; no harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.
- Follow the Acceptable Use Policy and Security Policy (`docs/security/`).

## 4. Remote Work & BYOD
- See `byod-policy.md` and `remote-work-policy.md`.
- Company data stays on managed/encrypted storage; no personal backups of PII.

## 5. Data & Privacy
- Employees handle user PII under GDPR/DPDP; report incidents per Incident Response Policy.

## 6. Assets & Exit
- Hardware returned on exit (`asset-policy.md`); access revoked same day (`exit-policy.md`).

## 7. Comms
- Internal: Slack/email. Escalations: see `docs/ops/incident-playbook.md`.
