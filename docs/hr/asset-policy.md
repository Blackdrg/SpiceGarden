# Asset Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0

## Issuance
- Laptops, peripherals, and access tokens issued against signed acknowledgement.

## Care
- Company assets for business use; report loss/theft within 24 h.

## Return
- On exit (`exit-policy.md`); missing assets deducted per contract/law.

## Disposal
- Wiped (cryptographic erase) before redeploy/recycle; logged.
