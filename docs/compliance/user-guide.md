# User Privacy Guide

Every end user can exercise their rights from the **Privacy Dashboard**
(`/privacy-dashboard` in customer-web) and the **cookie banner**.

## Cookie Consent

On first visit (EU/India), a banner appears with categories:
Necessary (locked), Functional, Preferences, Performance, Analytics, Marketing.
Choose **Manage Preferences** for granular control, **Reject Non-Essential**, or
**Accept All**. Withdraw any time from the dashboard.

## Privacy Dashboard

- **Your Data** — export as JSON / CSV / PDF (`/privacy/exports`). Downloads are
  generated on demand and streamed securely.
- **Consent & Cookies** — toggle each category; withdraw all non-essential.
- **Data Subject Requests** — submit Access, Correction, Restriction, Objection
  (GDPR) or Delete (GDPR/DPDP). Track status in the dashboard.
- **Grievance Officer (DPDP)** — contact details shown for India residents.

## Merchant & Driver

- Merchant: `restaurant-dashboard → Legal & Agreements`.
- Driver: `delivery-partner → Settings → Legal & Agreements` (accept Driver
  Agreement, view policies, DPDP rights).
