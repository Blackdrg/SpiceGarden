# Responsible Disclosure Policy

## Reporting
Report vulnerabilities to security@spicegarden.com with PGP encryption. See the Security Center for the key.

## Embargo
We request a 90-day coordinated disclosure embargo after resolution.

## Safe Harbor
Good-faith researchers complying with this policy will not face legal action.
