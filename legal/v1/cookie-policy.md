# Cookie Policy

## What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files used to operate, secure, and improve the service.

## Cookie Categories
Necessary (always on), Functional, Performance, Analytics, Marketing, Preference. Non-essential categories require consent.

## Cookie Registry
A current list of cookies is maintained and available via the Consent Center.

## Managing Cookies
You can grant or withdraw consent at any time in the Consent Center or via your browser.
