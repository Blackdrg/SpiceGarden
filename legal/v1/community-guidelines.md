# Community Guidelines

## Respect
Treat customers, restaurants, and delivery partners with respect. Harassment is prohibited.

## Safety
Do not request unsafe conduct or share harmful content.

## Authenticity
Provide truthful information in reviews and communications.

## Enforcement
Violations may lead to content removal, warnings, or account suspension.
