# Cancellation Policy

## Customer Cancellation
Customers may cancel before restaurant acceptance for a full refund. Later cancellations may incur fees per the Refund Policy.

## Restaurant Cancellation
Restaurants may cancel for operational reasons; repeated cancellations affect rating and may trigger penalties.

## Driver Cancellation
Drivers may cancel per the Driver Agreement; misuse affects incentives and standing.
