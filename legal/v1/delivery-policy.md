# Delivery Policy

## Service Areas
Delivery is available within mapped coverage areas that may change without notice.

## Delivery Fees
Fees are calculated by distance, demand, and promotional terms shown at checkout.

## Delivery Times
Estimated times are not guarantees. Delays from weather or traffic are outside our control.

## Handoff
Customers should provide accurate drop locations and be available to receive orders.
