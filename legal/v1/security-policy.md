# Security Policy

## Overview
We maintain defense-in-depth controls across network, application, and data layers.

## Encryption
Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).

## Access Control
RBAC, least privilege, MFA for administrative access, and just-in-time elevation.

## Monitoring
Centralized logging, audit trails, and anomaly detection. See the Security Center.
