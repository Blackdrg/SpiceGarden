# Driver Agreement

## Independent Contractor
Delivery partners are independent contractors, not employees.

## Insurance
Partners must maintain valid vehicle and third-party insurance.

## Code of Conduct
Partners must follow safety, hygiene, and conduct standards.

## Vehicle Requirements
Vehicles must be roadworthy, registered, and meet local requirements.

## Background Verification
Identity and background checks are conducted before activation.

## GPS and Location Tracking
Partners consent to location tracking during shifts for dispatch, safety, and payout verification.

## Payment Terms
Earnings are calculated per the payout policy and settled on the agreed cycle.

## Termination
Either party may terminate; outstanding dues are settled per this agreement.
