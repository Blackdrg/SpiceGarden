# Accessibility Statement

## Commitment
We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA across web and mobile apps.

## Measures
Semantic markup, keyboard navigation, contrast, and screen-reader testing.

## Feedback
Accessibility issues can be reported to accessibility@spicegarden.com.
