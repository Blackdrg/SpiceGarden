# Data Retention Policy

## Principle
We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes collected or as required by law.

## Retention Schedule
Orders 10y, Invoices 10y, Audit Logs 3y, Sessions 90d, OTP 24h, Driver GPS 30d, Deleted Accounts 7y, Analytics 18m.

## Disposal
Expired data is archived or permanently deleted via scheduled retention jobs; legal holds suspend deletion.
