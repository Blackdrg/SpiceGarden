# Acceptable Use Policy

## Permitted Use
Use the platform lawfully for its intended purpose.

## Prohibited
No scraping, reverse engineering, fraud, abuse, or circumvention of controls.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in throttling, suspension, or termination and referral to authorities.
